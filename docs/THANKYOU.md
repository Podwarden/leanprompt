# Thank you

People who shared a lesson learned, and what it taught leanprompt.

## How credit works

- Credit is optional. Share a lesson with the [issue form](https://github.com/Podwarden/leanprompt/issues/new?template=lesson-learned.yml) and leave the credit fields empty, and nothing about you is listed.
- If you want credit, give a name. If you also want to promote a project, give its name and a link.
- Maintainers add the entry here, after review of your submission. A submission that a maintainer has not reviewed is not listed.
- A project link is shown as given, without endorsement: listing it does not mean leanprompt or the LM Warden Team vouches for the project.
- Contributions are shared under CC BY 4.0. Submitted text is evidence that maintainers read; it is not pasted into a skill.

What to leave out of a lesson, and how it is reviewed: [CONTRIBUTING.md](../.github/CONTRIBUTING.md#share-a-lesson-learned).
